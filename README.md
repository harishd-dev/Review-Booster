# Review Booster – Smart Review Suggestion Page

## Overview

**Review Booster** is a lightweight web application designed to help customers quickly leave reviews by providing **natural, pre-written review suggestions**.

The page generates **realistic review text dynamically**, allowing users to copy and paste it into the review platform with minimal effort.

This improves the chances of customers actually leaving a review while keeping the experience fast and simple.

The page also contains a **link to my personal portfolio**, allowing visitors to explore my work.

## Key Features

• **Dynamic Review Generator** – Generates unique review suggestions every time the page loads
• **Randomized Text Variations** – Prevents repetitive or robotic-looking reviews
• **One-click Copy** – Users can quickly copy the suggested review text
• **QR Code Access** – Customers can scan and open the review page instantly
• **Minimal UI** – Simple design optimized for quick interaction
• **Mobile Friendly** – Works smoothly on smartphones and tablets

---

## How It Works

The review generator creates natural-looking reviews by combining random sentence components such as:

• Opening phrases
• Store context
• Staff assistance descriptions
• Product mentions
• Closing sentiment

This approach allows the system to generate **thousands of unique review variations** while keeping the text natural and human-like.

Example generated review:

> Had a great experience at Decathlon today. Harish helped me choose the right knee support. Very friendly service.

Another example:

> Visited Decathlon and Harish assisted me in finding a comfortable pair of running shoes. Really helpful staff.
